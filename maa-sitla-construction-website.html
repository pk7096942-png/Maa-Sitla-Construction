About Maa Sitla Construction

Maa Sitla Construction is a construction and infrastructure service business serving clients in Bhagalpur, Bihar and surrounding areas. We bring a practical, professional approach to construction requirements of different scales.

Our service scope includes sentring and shuttering work, building construction, RCC work, roads, bridges, infrastructure development and construction-material supply. We focus on clear coordination and strong construction solutions from discussion through execution.
